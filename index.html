Send to Frame Home

Your photos appear on the TV and join Your photos, at full size.

Send a link

In Google Photos or Google Drive, open the photo, tap Share, turn on link sharing if it asks, then Copy link and paste it here. The TV downloads the original. One link per line; a Google Photos album link sends the album.

Or send small photos

Photos up to 2 MB can go straight from your phone. Bigger ones: share a link instead.

    Links and small photos pass through ntfy.sh, a free relay that deletes them within 3 hours. Only the TV showing this page's code receives them. A link must be shared as “anyone with the link”; the TV keeps the photo, not the link.
