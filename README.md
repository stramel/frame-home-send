# Send to Frame Home

The page your phone opens when you scan the code on Frame Home's
**Settings › Art › Add your images › From your phone** screen.

It sends the photos you pick, at full size and quality (HEIC becomes a full-resolution JPEG,
since the TV can't show HEIC), to the [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh) mailbox named in the link (the
part after `#`). ntfy.sh takes up to 15 MB a photo. The TV is watching
that mailbox, saves each photo, and forgets the mailbox when you leave the screen. ntfy.sh
deletes the files after 3 hours. This page holds no keys and stores nothing.

Published with GitHub Pages at https://stramel.github.io/frame-home-send/.
